The Contests mode (#77)

Runs on its own when the page opens, in the frame beside the results, which stays in view as they scroll; takes about a minute, two of them Speed runs. Keep this tab in front.

Running…

    By hand, in the lab itself

    • Settings → Contests → Apply: the panel sits in the middle, clear of the HUD and the control bar, and reads well.
    • Speed's page: Run stands out as the thing to press.
    • During a run, nothing is drawn behind the panel, the clock's arc empties smoothly over the 6 seconds, and the chart climbs and rescales its generations axis as the run passes each step.
    • Hovering the chart shows a crosshair and the time and generations of the nearest point; its labels never overlap or run off the edge, at full width and at phone width.
    • The results card looks good in a screenshot: the score leads, the chart follows, the rest is secondary.
    • Export result: Chrome and Edge ask where to save speed-1-N.json (speed-1-1000-Tms.json at the ceiling); Firefox and Safari download it. node scripts/verify.mjs speed-1-N.json says verified, with the score.
    • The results card's browser line is right in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
    • At phone width (devtools), the panel sits above the control bar and scrolls if it must.
    • The GPU toggle's popover says contests run on the CPU.
    • Watch: the run plays back in 3D as in Explore, the camera follows the pattern as it grows, and past the render budget the chunks show as boxes. Dragging the scrub bar back and forth feels responsive, and the view settles on the generation let go at.
    • Watch's bar sits above the control bar, clear of it and of the HUD, at full width and at phone width.
    • Submit by email, in Chrome, Firefox and Safari with a mail app set up: Open the email is greyed out until Save result file has saved the file (Chrome asks where); then a new email opens to lab@morpheustech.net, with the subject and the text filled in, line breaks kept, and the saved file is still there; where mailto: goes to webmail, it opens in a new tab and the lab stays on its results card (#140).
    • The Copy buttons beside the address, the subject and the text each put it on the clipboard; pasted into a webmail compose window, the text keeps its lines.
    • The Submit section and the Hall of Fame (staged by the check above, and the real one in the lab itself) read well at full width and at phone width: long names end in an ellipsis, and Watch stays on the row.
    • Methuselah's page: the seed's picture reads as cubes in a box, in Methuselah's violet, for one cell, a small design and a full 10³ box; the entry, the four endings and the facts read well at full width and at phone width.
    • A long Methuselah run (Explore's Spreading noise preset, saved with Save seed and loaded here): the ring fills towards 10,000, the live-cells chart grows and rescales, and Stop answers at once. A run that settles late shows the whole history on its results card.
    • Export a Methuselah result, then node scripts/verify.mjs methuselah-1-N.json: verified, "settled at generation N (period P)" or "died at generation N".
    • Classic Methuselah, in Watch: a ship visibly leaves through the arena's wall at the generation the scrub bar's tick marks, and the departures line changes as it does, scrubbing back and forth too.
    • Classic Methuselah's page reads well at full width and at phone width, its five endings and five facts included; its card on the list sits beside Methuselah's.
