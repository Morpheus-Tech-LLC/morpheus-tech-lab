Runs on its own when the page opens, in the frame below; takes about 40 seconds, two of them Speed runs. Keep this tab in front.

Running…

    By hand, in the lab itself

    • Settings → Contests → Apply: the panel sits in the middle, clear of the HUD and the control bar, and reads well.
    • Speed's page: Run stands out as the thing to press.
    • During a run, nothing is drawn behind the panel, the clock's arc empties smoothly over the 6 seconds, and the chart climbs and rescales its generations axis as the run passes each step.
    • Hovering the chart shows a crosshair and the time and generations of the nearest point; its labels never overlap or run off the edge, at full width and at phone width.
    • The results card looks good in a screenshot: the score leads, the chart follows, the rest is secondary.
    • Export result: Chrome and Edge ask where to save speed-1-N.json; Firefox and Safari download it. node scripts/verify.mjs speed-1-N.json says verified, with the score.
    • The results card's browser line is right in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
    • At phone width (devtools), the panel sits above the control bar and scrolls if it must.
    • The GPU toggle's popover says contests run on the CPU.
