The seed editor (#70)

Runs on its own when the page opens, in the frame below; takes about 40 seconds.

Running…

    By hand, in the lab (npm run dev, then http://localhost:5180)

    • Seed gear → Custom → Design → Design…: the whole 10³ box shows, live cells as cubes, empty places as faint dots, its outline, and the cursor with red, green and blue guides.
    • Drag to turn the view; Front, Top, Side and Angled put the camera there. From each, the arrow keys move the cursor the way they point, and Page Up moves it away from you.
    • Click a live cube, then an empty dot: the cursor goes there. With 4 Box, Shift+click sets the box's other corner.
    • The selection is a yellow box: its live cells are tinted, its empty places bright. F fades every cell outside the plane or box, and a click then picks only inside it.
    • Export: the browser asks where to save (Chrome, Edge) or downloads explore-seed.json. Load… in the editor, or the Seed box's File → Load… then Apply, then Design…, opens it unchanged.
    • Save seed (Seed box) of a design in use, loaded back the same ways, opens unchanged.
    • Keyboard alone: Tab from the Seed gear through the popover to Design…, Enter opens the editor; Tab reaches every panel button; Space on a focused button presses it, Space on the view switches the selection; Esc closes and focus is back on the gear.
    • At phone width (devtools, 375 px), the panel sits at the foot and scrolls, the box shows above it, and the on-screen arrows, Away and Near move the cursor.
    • While the editor is open, a running run keeps counting (close the editor: the generation has moved on).
